# Business Insights from Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## Overview

This project analyzes six months of transactional sales data from a coffee shop business operating across multiple store locations.

Using Power BI, the objective was to transform raw sales data into an interactive dashboard that provides insights into sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product demand, and store-level performance.

The analysis combines KPI monitoring, product analysis, customer behavior patterns, and operational insights to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## Key Business Questions

The analysis focuses on answering the following questions:

* How is overall sales performance evolving over time?
* Which stores generate the highest sales and transaction volume?
* Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
* What are the peak shopping days and hours?
* How does product pricing influence transaction quantity?
* Which products are the best and worst performers?
* What opportunities exist to increase sales and operational efficiency?

---

## Key Insights

### 1. Sales Show Strong Growth Throughout the Reporting Period

The business generated:

* Total Sales: €726,866
* Total Quantity Sold: 214,470
* Total Transactions: 149,116

**Key observations**

* Monthly sales increased significantly from January to June.
* June achieved the highest sales performance.
* Most months exceeded average sales levels.

**Business implication**

The positive sales trend indicates growing customer demand and provides opportunities for expansion, inventory optimization, and targeted promotions during high-demand periods.

---

### 2. Coffee and Tea Are the Main Revenue Drivers

Product category analysis shows that Coffee and Tea contribute the largest share of revenue.

**Key observations**

* Coffee generated approximately €253K in sales.
* Tea generated approximately €183K in sales.
* Together, these categories account for more than half of total revenue.
* Categories such as Packaged Chocolate and Flavours contribute relatively little revenue.

**Business implication**

Management should prioritize inventory availability and promotional activities for Coffee and Tea while evaluating the profitability of smaller product categories.

---

### 3. Store Performance Is Relatively Balanced

Store analysis indicates that sales are distributed relatively evenly across locations.

**Key observations**

* Astoria generates the highest sales contribution.
* Hell's Kitchen closely follows Astoria.
* Lower Manhattan contributes slightly less revenue.
* No single store dominates overall business performance.

**Business implication**

Balanced store performance reduces operational risk and suggests that successful practices can be replicated across locations.

---

### 4. Customer Demand Peaks During Morning Hours

Shopper behavior analysis reveals clear purchasing patterns throughout the day.

**Key observations**

* Highest transaction activity occurs between approximately 8:00 AM and 11:00 AM.
* Sales activity declines during afternoon and evening hours.
* Weekdays generate stronger customer traffic than weekends.

**Business implication**

Staff scheduling, inventory preparation, and promotional campaigns should be optimized around morning peak periods to improve service quality and operational efficiency.

---

### 5. Product Price Has Limited Impact on Purchase Frequency

Analysis of unit price versus transaction quantity shows no strong negative relationship between price and demand.

**Key observations**

* Higher-priced products continue to generate strong transaction volumes.
* Customer demand appears to be driven by product preference rather than price alone.
* Several premium products maintain healthy sales performance.

**Business implication**

The business may have opportunities to introduce premium offerings or apply selective price increases without significantly reducing demand.

---

### 6. A Small Number of Products Generate a Significant Share of Revenue

Product-level analysis highlights clear differences in performance.

**Key observations**

* Cappuccino, Latte, and premium coffee products rank among the best sellers.
* Top-performing products consistently outperform monthly averages.
* Bottom-performing products contribute only a small portion of total revenue.

**Business implication**

Marketing efforts and inventory planning should focus on high-performing products, while underperforming items should be reviewed for pricing, placement, or potential removal.

---

### 7. Monthly Performance Varies Across Product Categories

Month-over-month analysis shows that some categories grow faster than others.

**Key observations**

* Coffee Beans and Branded products achieved the strongest growth rates.
* Most categories reported positive growth compared with the previous month.
* Product category performance is not uniform across the portfolio.

**Business implication**

Understanding category-specific growth trends helps management allocate resources toward products with the highest growth potential.

---

## Conclusion

This Power BI analysis demonstrates how sales and customer behavior data can be transformed into actionable business insights.

Key takeaways:

* Sales increased steadily throughout the six-month period.
* Coffee and Tea are the primary revenue drivers.
* Store performance is balanced across locations.
* Morning hours represent the most valuable sales period.
* Product demand is relatively resilient to price differences.
* A small number of products contribute a large share of revenue.
* Product categories show different growth patterns and opportunities.

These insights highlight the value of Power BI-based sales analysis in understanding customer behavior, improving operational efficiency, and supporting strategic business decisions.
