# :bar_chart: Coffee Shop Sales Analysis (Power BI Project)
🔗 Link to access my dashboard: https://lnkd.in/gQEKes8n
## Project Overview

Built an interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze coffee shop sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product demand, and store-level trends, generating actionable business insights to support decision-making.

The project uses six months of transactional sales data and combines Power Query transformations, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visualizations to monitor key business KPIs and identify growth opportunities.

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains coffee shop transaction data from January to June across multiple store locations.

Main tables used in the analysis:

* Sales Transactions
* Product Information
* Store Information

Key fields include:

* Transaction Date
* Product
* Product Category
* Unit Price
* Quantity Sold
* Sales Revenue
* Store Location

The dataset enables analysis of sales performance, customer purchasing patterns, store contribution, and product demand.

## Data Preparation

Data preparation was performed using Power Query.

### Key Transformation Steps

A custom Date Table was created to support time intelligence analysis.

The Date Table includes:

* Year
* Month
* Month Name
* Weekday
* Day Number
* Calendar attributes

This structure enables month-over-month comparisons, trend analysis, and peak sales period identification.

## Business Questions

The analysis focuses on answering the following questions:

* How is overall sales performance evolving over time?
* Which stores generate the highest sales and transaction volume?
* Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
* What are the peak shopping days and hours?
* How does product pricing influence transaction quantity?
* Which products are the best and worst performers?
* What opportunities exist to increase sales and operational efficiency?

---

## Key Business Insights

### Sales Growth

* Sales increased consistently throughout the reporting period.
* June recorded the highest monthly sales performance.
* Most months exceeded average sales levels.

### Product Performance

* Coffee and Tea generate the largest share of revenue.
* A small number of products contribute a significant portion of total sales.
* Several low-performing products contribute minimal revenue.

### Store Performance

* Sales are relatively balanced across all store locations.
* Astoria and Hell's Kitchen generate slightly higher sales than Lower Manhattan.

### Shopper Behavior

* Customer demand peaks during morning hours.
* Weekdays generate stronger transaction activity than weekends.
* Product demand appears relatively resilient to price differences.
